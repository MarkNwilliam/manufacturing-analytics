About me

I am a Chemical Engineer and Production Officer at Quality Chemical Industries Limited, a WHO prequalified pharmaceutical plant in Uganda, where I run solid dosage manufacturing under cGMP and turn the daily floor numbers into decisions. My work covers granulation, drying, milling, blending and compression, in process quality control, batch manufacturing records and line clearance, deviation and corrective action follow up, purified water systems and equipment qualification, with a batch approval rate around 98 percent.

This portfolio is the toolset I wished I had on the floor. Every project is a dependency free Python library with a unit test suite and a live dashboard, and each one encodes the real method rather than demo filler, from OEE decomposition and USP limits to the Carr and Hausner flow table and the Heckel compaction models. I also build software and AI products, and I read Chemical Engineering at Kyambogo University.
